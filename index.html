Bringing a whole team into AI-powered design

At Decoded I redesigned our learning design workflow around Claude Design and Claude Code, then brought fellow designers and subject experts along with me, so the whole team could create e-learning and workshop materials at a pace that wasn't possible before.

I built a set of custom skills that encode how good learning is made: the structure of a storyboard, the shape of a lesson plan, the standards a slide deck has to meet, and the checks that catch problems before a learner does. These are shared skills, used and improved by the team rather than kept as one person's toolkit.

I ran sessions to share the skills, paired with colleagues on live projects, and worked closely with subject experts so their knowledge shaped every output. The AI does the heavy lifting on production; people make the learning design decisions about what learners need to do, how they practise it, and how we know it worked.

LT Gold 2023 · BETT 2024

ICE CityZen

A team-based city-builder game for the Institution of Civil Engineers. Students build and maintain a 3D city, make meaningful trade-offs, and compete on a countrywide scoreboard.

Deployed in 279 UK secondary schools for five years, with a sharp rise in engagement with civil engineering careers.

LT Gold 2025

NHSBT Crossmatching

A detailed VR simulation of a complex blood transfusion lab procedure for NHS Blood and Transplant, with accurate instruments and multiple outcomes.

Equivalent or better outcomes than one-to-one training, with large time and cost savings. Published at ISBT and SHOT in 2023.

LT Silver 2022

An immersive experience that raises awareness of microaggressions and inclusive hiring, using perspective switching, branching dialogue and gamified interactions.

In the following 12 months EDF doubled recruitment of ethnic minority candidates and halved attrition.

EDF Sizewell C Visitor Centre

A major physical installation that tells the story of a nuclear power station to the public. I led the design from the winning pitch to opening day in January 2026.

Brief
Give visitors to a nuclear new-build a clear, engaging and accessible understanding of what's being built and why.
Approach
A physical space with a deliberate visitor journey and narrative, tactile exhibits, digital games and a VR experience, with accessibility designed in from the start.
Outcome
Opened January 2026. Make Real's first major physical installation, which I won and led.
